Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our rates, billing, and booking policies.

Are there any hidden fees or extra charges on the day?

None at all. All our quotes and package prices are 100% transparent flat rates. We only charge extra if you explicitly request an optional add-on or if your home size substantially exceeds what was booked.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or vacuum cleaners?

No, our certified cleaners arrive fully equipped with professional-grade vacuums, microfibers, extendable dusters, and eco-friendly cleaning detergents. You don't have to provide a single thing!

How does the 100% Satisfaction Guarantee work?

If any corner or task was not cleaned up to our exacting standards, let us know within 24 hours of completion. We will dispatch a cleaner back to your home to re-clean the disputed area completely free of charge.

When and how do I pay?

We accept all major credit and debit cards online via our secure 256-bit encrypted checkout. Your card is pre-authorized at booking, and only charged after your cleaning has been completed to your satisfaction.
