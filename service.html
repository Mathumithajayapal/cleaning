Our Expertise

A Closer Look At Our Core Services

We don't just skim the surface. Below is a detailed breakdown of what is included in our top-tier housekeeping packages, ensuring you know exactly what to expect from the CleanHome team.

Most Popular

Regular Housekeeping & Maintenance

Designed for busy professionals and families, our regular cleaning service keeps your home in pristine condition week after week. We focus on high-traffic areas and essential upkeep.

  • Living Areas: Dusting all surfaces, vacuuming carpets, mopping hard floors, emptying trash, and tidying up loose items.
  • Bedrooms: Making beds, dusting furniture, wiping mirrors, and thorough floor care.
  • General: Wiping down door handles, light switches, and basic sanitization to reduce household allergens.

Recommended Bi-Annually

Comprehensive Deep Cleaning

When standard cleaning isn't enough, our deep clean targets the forgotten, hard-to-reach areas. Perfect for spring cleaning or preparing for major events.

  • Detailed Dusting: Ceiling fans, air vents, door frames, baseboards, and intricate light fixtures.
  • Heavy Scrubbing: Spot-cleaning walls, removing scuff marks, and intensive interior window washing.
  • Hidden Spaces: Moving light furniture to clean underneath, and vacuuming upholstery deeply.
Book Deep Clean
Intensive Sanitization

Dedicated Kitchen & Bath Detail

Kitchens and bathrooms harbor the most bacteria and tough stains. We deploy specialized, non-toxic products to cut through grease, soap scum, and hard water stains.

  • Kitchen Degreasing: Scrubbing stovetops, range hoods, backsplashes, and the exterior (and optional interior) of appliances.
  • Bathroom Descaling: Removing mineral deposits from showerheads and faucets. Intensive grout scrubbing and sanitizing tubs and showers.
  • Disinfection: Complete wipe-down of countertops, sinks, and toilets to guarantee a hygienic, germ-free environment.
Book Kitchen & Bath Detail
Stress-Free Moving

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Moving is stressful enough without having to worry about cleaning. Whether ensuring you get your deposit back or preparing a fresh canvas for your new chapter, we handle it all.

  • Cabinet & Drawer Interiors: Wiping out all empty shelving units, closets, and built-in storage.
  • Appliance Interiors: Deep cleaning inside the oven, refrigerator, and microwave to remove all food residues.
  • Complete Overhaul: Includes all Deep Clean checklist items, ensuring the property looks as good as new.
