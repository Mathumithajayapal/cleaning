Our Story

How CleanHome Began

Founded in 2015, CleanHome started with a simple mission: to give busy families their weekends back. We realized that maintaining a pristine home was becoming a luxury of time that few could afford.

What began as a small operation with just two cleaners has blossomed into a city-wide trusted service. Our dedication to meticulous detail, eco-friendly products, and unwavering customer satisfaction remains the cornerstone of everything we do.
