Help & FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions about our cleaning processes, pricing, and scheduling policies below.

General Services

Not at all! Our professional teams arrive fully equipped with premium, eco-friendly cleaning supplies and commercial-grade vacuums. If you prefer us to use specific products you own, simply let us know in advance.

Yes, we are pet-friendly! We use non-toxic products safe for animals. We just ask that aggressive or highly anxious pets be secured in a separate room or crate for their safety and our cleaners' efficiency.

Standard cleaning covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping floors, emptying trash, and thorough cleaning of bathrooms and kitchen surfaces. For inside ovens, fridges, or windows, please request a Deep Clean.

Pricing & Payments

Our pricing is transparent and based primarily on the square footage of your home and the specific type of service requested (Standard, Deep, or Move-In/Out). You can get an instant quote on our booking page.

Tipping is entirely optional but always appreciated by our hardworking staff. If you choose to tip, you can give cash directly to your cleaner or add a tip during your online checkout process.

A hold is placed on your credit card the day before your appointment, but you won't be charged until the cleaning is completely finished. We accept all major credit cards and digital wallets.

Booking & Scheduling

It's completely up to you! Most of our clients prefer to provide a spare key, a door code, or leave a key in a lockbox. We will lock up carefully after we finish the job.

We understand plans change. You can cancel or reschedule without any penalty up to 24 hours before your scheduled appointment. Cancellations made within 24 hours incur a $50 late-cancellation fee.

For recurring services, we make every effort to send the same cleaning team to your home so they learn your specific preferences. However, in cases of illness or vacation, we will send a reliable substitute.

Safety & Trust

Absolutely. Your peace of mind is our priority. Every cleaner undergoes a rigorous nationwide background check, in-person interviews, and comprehensive training. We are fully bonded and insured.

While we treat your home with the utmost care, accidents occasionally happen. We are fully insured, and if our staff damages anything, we will immediately report it, repair it, or cover the replacement cost.

Client keys are stored in a secure lockbox at our office and only checked out on the day of service. We maintain strict protocols for alarm codes, which are encrypted within our secure booking system.

Quality & Guarantees

We stand firmly behind our work. If you are not completely satisfied with an area we cleaned, let us know within 24 hours. We will promptly return and re-clean the specific area for free.

After each visit, you'll receive an automated email asking you to rate your experience out of 5 stars. You can also leave detailed feedback through your customer portal to help us continuously improve.
